# envs117-final-project

Project idea: Study which orchard (such as almond) areas in the Central Valley may be vulnerable in the future to land-use change due to water scarcity (groundwater level trends), the economics of water (price of water per almond), and the economics of growing orchards (such as revenue from almonds).
Study Area: Central Valley, California             (This topic relates to Hydrology)
One Possible Data Source: U.S. Geological Survey. (n.d.). National groundwater monitoring network. National Integrated Drought Information System / National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration. Retrieved October 11, 2026, from https://www.drought.gov/data-maps-tools/national-groundwater-monitoring-network
